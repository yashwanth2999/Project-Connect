About ProjectConnect

ProjectConnect is an exclusive platform designed to bridge the gap between students, innovative ideas, and successful team execution in the academic landscape. Our mission is to eliminate the cycle of repetitive, plagiarized college projects and foster a culture of genuine innovation and cross-disciplinary collaboration.

Idea Discovery

Explore unique, domain-specific project ideas pitched by peers. Stop recycling old projects and start building something that matters.

Smart Team Formation

Find teammates with the exact complementary skills you need. Post openings and recruit across different departments and years.

Unified Workspace

Collaborate in real-time with integrated group chats, virtual video meetings, and task tracking—all without leaving the platform.

Why We Built This

We believe every student deserves a team that matches their energy. Too often, great ideas are lost because students don't know who has the right coding, design, or research skills in their college. ProjectConnect was built by students, for students, to make finding a co-founder for your final-year or mini-project as easy as sending a text.
